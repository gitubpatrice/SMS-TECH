SMS Tech is an ad-free, tracker-free SMS / MMS app for Android. It replaces the stock messaging app while keeping every message on your device — no cloud, no analytics, no third-party SDK shipping your data anywhere.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li>SMS and MMS — full default-SMS-app support on Android 8 (API 26) through 15+</li>
<li>Voice messages — push-to-talk recording sent as a real MMS, capped at 60 s / 300 KB so every carrier accepts it</li>
<li>Encrypted vault — hide sensitive conversations behind SQLCipher, with the key wrapped in the Android Keystore. Toggle "Lock vault on leave" to re-lock it every time the app goes to background</li>
<li>Panic code — a second unlock PIN that opens the app in a decoy view where the vault stays invisible and inaccessible</li>
<li>Emergency mode — hold the button for 3 s to send an alert SMS to your Safety Call contacts, with a single GPS fix if you granted location; without that permission the SMS still goes out, only without coordinates. Quick access to 112 / 15 / 17 / 18, and shortcuts on the lockscreen notification. Placing the call directly is opt-in: by default the dialer simply opens pre-filled</li>
<li>Biometric unlock — fingerprint or face, with a PIN fallback you set yourself</li>
<li>Contextual reply — long-press a message, pick "Reply" and your composer shows a mini-quote so the recipient sees what you are answering</li>
<li>Delegated translation — long-press a message and pick "Translate" to hand the text to whichever translation app you have installed (Google Translate, DeepL, Aves Translate …) through a system Intent. No translation model is bundled, and SMS Tech itself sends nothing anywhere</li>
<li>Scheduled messages — write an SMS, pick a date and time, and it is sent at that moment even if the app is closed</li>
<li>OS-wide blocked numbers — at first launch, SMS Tech mirrors the system blocklist (Phone app, Samsung Messages) and retroactively deletes the conversations that match</li>
<li>Attachment picker — Photo, Video, File, Contact card. Photos are automatically re-encoded as JPEG to fit MMS carrier caps</li>
<li>Full-text search across every conversation, with FTS4 indexing</li>
<li>Export a conversation as a PDF for archival</li>
<li>Encrypted local backup using a passphrase distinct from your unlock PIN</li>
<li>Themes — Light / Dark / AMOLED / "Dark Tech" slate-blue accent</li>
<li>French and English UI</li>
<li>Confirmation toggle in Settings → Send → "Confirm before send" requires a confirmation dialog before any SMS, MMS or attachment is dispatched</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>

<ul>
<li>No analytics, no crash reporters, no third-party tracking SDK</li>
<li>Only network usage: your carrier's MMSC, for MMS transport. Nothing else leaves the phone</li>
<li>Location and phone-call permissions serve Emergency mode only. Both are opt-in, neither is ever used in the background, and the app works without granting either</li>
