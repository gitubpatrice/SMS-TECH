New: select several conversations, then archive them, move them to the vault or delete them in one go, from a bar of labelled actions at the bottom of the list. An emergency alert, a Safety Call or an "I am OK" message that fails on the chosen SIM is now sent again through the other SIM, and the emergency screen says while the alert is being sent. A long SMS counts as sent only once all its parts are out. Build tools updated, including Gradle 9.9.1 (security fixes).
