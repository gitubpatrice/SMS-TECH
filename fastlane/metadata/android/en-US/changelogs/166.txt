v1.25.3 — Blocking no longer deletes, scheduled sends fixed

• IMPORTANT: blocking a conversation no longer deletes it. It
  stays at the bottom of the list, marked in orange, and comes
  back if you unblock. An automatic purge used to delete it,
  with no warning.
• Scheduled sends: a failure is retried again, and shows up in
  a "Failed" section where you can send it again.
• Biometric unlock is now backed by the Keystore: Class 3 only,
  vault included.
• "About" screen fully translated.
