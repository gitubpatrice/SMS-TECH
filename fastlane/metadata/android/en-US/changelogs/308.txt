Dual SIM: the SIM used is shown above the text field, tap it to change; a reply goes by the SIM of the last message. Settings > Sending > SIM for sending covers automatic messages. Tap a message to see its time and status; the last one sent in a series shows them. Fixed: blank conversation list at launch; received SMS dated by the carrier, sometimes an hour off; a sent message missing from its conversation, or a received SMS lost.
