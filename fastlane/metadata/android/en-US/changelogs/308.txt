Dual SIM: the SIM used is shown above the text field, tap it to change. A conversation answers by the SIM of its last message, from the notification too. Settings > Sending > SIM for sending covers automatic messages. A removed SIM no longer makes sending fail. Fixed: a sent message could appear in the conversation list but not in the conversation, and a received SMS could be lost, when Android reused an older message's address or refused to keep a copy.
