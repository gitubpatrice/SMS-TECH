Dual SIM: the SIM used is shown above the text field, tap it to change; a reply goes by the SIM of the last message. Tap a message to see its time and status. A received MMS that failed to download is retried, or by hand with Retry. New group: each contact added is shown and confirmed; a blocked member is announced before sending. Fixed: blank list at launch; received SMS an hour off; a sent message missing, or a received SMS lost.
